BICHROME is a boutique creative agency delivering strategy-led creative production for brands that are building something worth paying attention to.

Founded by Ashley Garcia-Maynard, a Creative Director and Producer with nearly a decade across entertainment, fintech, luxury travel, consumer, children's media, and lifestyle brands. Every engagement is anchored by senior creative strategy and direction. You get the thinking, the taste, and the oversight that keeps the work sharp, start to finish.

Our team delivers across every channel and format. The strategy, the vision, and the quality standard are always at the center.

How We Work

The process.

01

Intake & Alignment

Before any creative begins, we align on your goals, audience, brand voice, and what success actually looks like. No assumptions, no guessing.

02

Brief & Strategy

We develop a clear creative brief that everyone agrees on before production starts. This is the most important step — and where most agencies skip.

03

Production & QA

Creative is produced, reviewed, and QA'd against the brief before it ever reaches you. What you receive is polished, on-brand, and ready.

04

Delivery & Iteration

Final assets delivered to your shared library, organized and labeled. Monthly check-ins ensure the work keeps improving based on what's performing.
